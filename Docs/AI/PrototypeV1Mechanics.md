# Prototype V1 Mechanics

The prototype validates one complete combat loop: defend to build three charge, extract red from a world source, spend the one-use empowered attack, fill three red slots, then activate the eight-second red-mask form.

The enemy alternates a parryable attack with an orange unblockable attack. A successful parry grants two charge. A dodge grants one charge only when an active enemy hit overlaps the player's invulnerability window. Charge caps at three.

Extraction slows the scene to ten percent speed. Confirming a red source clears charge, fills one slot, and grants one empowered attack without consuming filled slots. The source turns gray for three seconds. Canceling costs nothing.

The empowered attack is selected by direction and grounded state: neutral is a dash slash, up is an uppercut, and airborne down is a dive slam. Grounded down remains a normal attack and preserves the empowered token.

At three red slots, R activates red-mask form, clears slots and any unused empowered token, and temporarily replaces attacks with faster, higher-poise-damage versions. Charge cannot be earned and extraction cannot begin during the form.

Victory, defeat, restart, component disable, and scene exit always restore normal time.
